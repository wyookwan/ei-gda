## Important interpretation rule
The catalog stores normalized Ei-GDA assessment statements. It does not reproduce proprietary ISO clause text. A mapped source means “relevant to this Test Suite”; it does not mean every legal obligation applies to every organization or AI system. Applicability must be evaluated by jurisdiction, sector, role, system type and use case.
